Themindcurator is a solo web design studio. One founder designs and builds every website himself, with a manager who never sleeps — so a small studio delivers with the discipline of a big one.

What "curate" means

Anyone can put a business online. Curation means choosing — which photos lead, which words stay, which sections earn their place — until what remains is the version of your business people remember. Nothing generic survives that process.

How we work

Every project starts with your business, not a template. We study what you do, design around it, build the site in our own code, and check it on a real phone before you ever see it. Then it goes live — and stays yours.
