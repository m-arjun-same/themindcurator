Alessandro Pet Spa

The Brief.

A Los Angeles pet grooming spa, in business since 2005, needed a website that showed the grooming difference instead of just listing services.

The Build.

We built a site around one idea — the reveal. Visitors drag a layer and watch a shaggy dog become a groomed dog, walk through a day at the spa, check daycare rates, and book an appointment. The store's real location sits on a map inside the booking section.
